Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Star City Sites respects the privacy of website visitors and prospective clients. This policy explains what information is collected through starcitysites.com, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information collected

When you submit a project inquiry or website-audit request, the site may collect your name, email address, business name, website address, selected service, budget range, and the details you voluntarily provide in the message field.

The hosting provider may also process routine technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, request timestamps, and security logs. This information is generally used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical issues.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a potential project, prepare an audit or proposal, provide requested services, maintain business records, and protect the website from fraud or misuse.

Star City Sites does not sell submitted personal information or add inquiry contacts to unrelated third-party marketing lists.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers used to host the website, route form submissions, provide business email, monitor security, or maintain operational records. These providers receive only the information needed to perform their service.

Data retention

Inquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized.

Cookies and analytics

The initial version of this website does not require advertising cookies. Basic privacy-oriented traffic analytics may be enabled later to understand aggregate site usage. This policy should be updated before introducing materially different tracking or advertising tools.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information previously submitted through this site, subject to legal and operational recordkeeping requirements.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect submitted information. No Internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised as the website, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to hello@starcitysites.com.
