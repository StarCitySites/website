Common questions

What to expect before getting started.

Have a different question? Include it in the project form and it can be addressed directly.

How long does a website project take?

A focused small-business site often takes two to six weeks once the required content and decisions are available. Larger projects are scheduled according to scope.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Project terms identify the deliverables and ownership clearly. Domain accounts should remain in your name, and you are not locked into a proprietary page-builder subscription.

Can you work with my existing domain?

Yes. Domains registered through GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, and similar providers can be connected or migrated without changing the public domain name.

Do you write the website content?

Content organization and editing are included at an appropriate level for each package. Full copywriting, photography, or brand development can be scoped separately when needed.

What happens after launch?

You receive launch support and can choose an ongoing hosting and care plan. A handoff option is also available for businesses that prefer to manage the site elsewhere.
